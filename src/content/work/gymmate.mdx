# GymMate: one app for the whole workout

GymMate is a workout tracker and warmup calculator I built for myself, without writing code, and now use every gym session. [Try the demo](https://warm-up-and-lift.lovable.app/demo), no account needed.

<Eyebrow>Why I built it</Eyebrow>

## Every app switch broke my focus

<Grid cols={2}>

<div>

Mid-workout, I was bouncing between a notes app to log sets, a spreadsheet to track progress and a calculator to figure out warmup plates. Every switch broke my focus between sets.

I wanted one app that remembers what I lifted last time, tells me exactly what to load for warmups, and gets out of the way.

<Eyebrow>How I built it</Eyebrow>

## Managed like a product, with AI as the engineering team

1. **Wrote a spec first.** A "prompt wizard": purpose, look and feel, required screens, user flows. I ran it through other AI tools to tighten it before building.
2. **Built, then used it at the gym.** Each workout surfaced something that broke or felt slow.
3. **Turned that into the next spec.** About 40 short iterations from May to October 2026, each one a focused list of fixes.

</Section>

<Section tone="light">

<Eyebrow>What it does</Eyebrow>

## Remembers, calculates, gets out of the way

<Grid cols={2} reveal="right">

<div>

- **Remembers your last session.** Add an exercise and it fills in last time's weight, sets and reps.
- **Calculates warmups.** Pick bench, squat or deadlift, enter your working weight, and it lays out each warmup set with the plates to load per side.
- **Routines and history.** Load a saved routine in one tap; check any exercise's history before you lift.
- **Works anywhere.** Syncs across phone and laptop, installs to your home screen, exports to a spreadsheet.

<Accordion title="Notable details">

- Defaults come from history: the most recent logged weight and set count override saved routine values.
- Warmup math is lift-specific: bench, squat and deadlift each use their own percentage ladder, rounded to loadable plates.
- Every input saves to the cloud within a second or two, with an offline queue.
- Demo mode runs entirely in the browser on sample data and never touches the database.
- Spreadsheet export is formatted by week and day, matching my old workout template.
- Accessibility pass against WCAG 2.2 AA.
