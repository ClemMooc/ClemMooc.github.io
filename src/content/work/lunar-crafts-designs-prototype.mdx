# Lunar Crafts & Designs

## Project Summary

Lunar Crafts & Designs is an e-commerce website I developed as a UX student at the School of Visual Concepts (SVC). **As a first-generation Asian American and as a child of Vietnam war refugees**, a pillar of my creativity is using UX design to celebrate Asian Americans.

Frustrated by my personal experience buying festival products like **gold burning paper (金紙)** and **red envelopes (紅包)**, I was determined to design a website that teaches individuals how to properly participate and prepare for traditional Chinese rituals and festivals.

<Eyebrow>Problem</Eyebrow>

## Beautiful festivals 🤝 Complicated learning process

<Rule />

### Learning about Chinese festivals is discouraging and overwhelming

Asian Americans are eager to embrace Chinese festivals, but often face challenges. <br /><br />Many lack access to valuable insights from older family members due to the Chinese to English **language barriers.** <br /><br />Online searches, unreliable translation apps, and cluttered guides force people to **sift through multiple sources to find a crumb of useful information.**<br /><br />When it’s time to purchase festival goods, local supermarkets offer limited Chinese products in a generic 'Ethnic Products' aisle. Voyaging over to a specialized store presents challenges with **confusing labels, disorganized shelves, and frequent stockouts.**

### 100+ data points lead to 4 key insights:

After concluding my quantitative and qualitative research. I determined the key motivations and pain points that users have regarding learning about Chinese festivals and purchasing festival goods.

### 01.<br />Motivated, but not confident

**91% of customers are motivated to celebrate their family heritage**, but... **<br /><br />67% of customers don’t feel confident** in hosting or participating in a festival.

### 02.<br />Confused by unclear source-of-truths

People are currently building their knowledge of Chinese festivals from a variety of camera translation apps, festival websites, and family members. <br /><br />These methods are confusing, and **users aren’t 100% confident they are getting the right answers.**

### 03.<br />Feeling lost in translation

The **English/Chinese language barrier was the #1 pain point** in learning about festivals.<br /><br />Festivals are inherently social, but language barriers stifle a significant touchpoint for learning and sharing Chinese traditions with friends and family members.<br /><br />When purchasing festival products, customers can't decipher the Chinese product packaging, which complicates the purchasing process further.

### 04.<br />Love supporting small businesses

**2 in 3 customers support the growth of minority cultures**, making it the most popular reason to purchase from a minority-owned business over a big-box retailer.

### 01. <br />The product page is disorganized.

Participants felt overwhelmed and unintentionally ignored items that would have helped them understand the product better.<br /><br />To remove cognitive load and confusion on the product page, I leveraged gestalt principles, clear and concise labeling, and teal color-coding to increase the saliency of features on the product page.

### Find what you need, discover what you want.

A great way to learn about a holiday or festival is to be able to discover new things on your own pace. Discoverability features provide customers a freedom of exploration, without getting lost.**<br /><br />Informative mega menu:** customers won't get lost learning new festivals with card-sort optimized navigation menus and breadcrumb navigations.**<br /><br />Product tags:** just like hashtags, customers can understand a product's attributes, and can click on them to continue their exploration for products with similar attributes.**<br /><br />Guides:** Want to learn more about a product? Related guides on the right will answer anything from product origins, how-tos, and symbolism.**<br /><br />Compatible festivals:** save stress and time by running a quick sanity check to make sure your product is compatible with your festival or event.**<br /><br />Don't know what pairs well?:** check out the related products or bundling suggestions to find your next move.

</div>

</Grid>

<Grid cols={2}>

<div>

### Dismantle the language barrier.

Lunar Crafts and Designs promotes conversations between Chinese and English speaking family members or friends while they are researching on the website. <br /><br />Whether you want to **translate the full page**, **translate just the product name**, or **learn how to pronounce** it for your family, customers won't have to know Chinese nor interpret for Chinese-speaking customers.

</div>

### Build confidence with intuitive learning resources.

LCD provides customers with evergreen resources that they can go back to and reference **before**, **during**, and even **after** their purchasing engagement.**<br /><br />Festival calendar:** follow the traditional Chinese calendars and outline upcoming Chinese festivals and holidays.**<br /><br />Festival wiki:** click on any product tile and learn about its origins, symbolism, etiquette, and other helpful information.**<br /><br />Festival info page:** learn more about each of the ten Chinese festivals. **<br /><br />Month-specific festival module:** customers can easily find the festival of the month on the home page and get started.

</div>

<div>

### Empower real AAPI businesses.

Customers can feel safe knowing they're not buying from a faceless entity, but buying from real AAPI businesses.<br /><br />Get to know who you’re buying from with **vendor pages** and **vendor spotlights**.<br /><br />Get to know what you're buying with real product descriptions from the vendor.<br /><br />Feel comfortable knowing you're buying **high-quality goods** with the same **buyer protection** as big-box retailers.

<Eyebrow>Reflections</Eyebrow>

This is my first end-to-end UX project. It was a tremendous learning experience and I learned so much about myself as a UX designer.

## The Product page is a sacred place

Speaking to research participants was an eye-opening experience. Each usability test taught me how much you do **NOT** know about your designs' usability. It's easy to assume until you discover something you never thought about. Therein lies the beauty of research, and to a larger degree, empathize with humans.

## Above-the-fold content sets the tone

It’s important to keep the user’s primary and secondary goals in mind when updating a page. Placing ancillary or supplementary content or items above the fold affects that real estate. It confuses participants who don’t know what the main use case or goal for the page is.
