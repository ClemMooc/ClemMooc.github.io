# Frank Ocean: By the Numbers

An infographic about the Grammy-winning singer-songwriter. I built each part around one principle of visual potential (appearance, numbers, location, relationships, time and layout), drafted two options for each, and let peer critique decide.

<Eyebrow>Final piece</Eyebrow>

## A circular timeline at the center, with everything else arranged around it

<Img src="frank-ocean/hero.png" alt="The finished infographic poster, 'The storied career of: Frank Ocean': a circular timeline of releases from 2005 to 2017, plus residency, accolades, works, collaborators, notable studios and a genre wheel." />

**Arrangement:** a top-down, horizontal layout with circular rings centered on "Frank Ocean." Leading lines carry the eye left to right from the start of the timeline.

**Grouping:** blocks above the center line cover Frank's biography; blocks below cover collaborators, genres and studio locations.

<Eyebrow>Process</Eyebrow>

## Two drafts per section, chosen by critique

Each section started as two options. Peers reviewed both, and I kept what read most clearly.

<Accordion title="Appearance: how to show who Frank is">

<Grid cols={2}>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/screen-shot-2019-01-29-at-5-30-09-am-edi.webp" alt="Option 1: detailed portrait with key stats" caption="Option 1: detailed portrait with a stat sheet." />

</div>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/appearance2.webp" alt="Option 2: simplified outline of Frank Ocean" caption="Option 2: simplified outline with bold red title." />

</div>

</Grid>

Peers found option 1 easy to read "like a stat sheet"; option 2 was more striking but its sharp outlines didn't sit well on the background.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Numbers: awards and certifications">

<Grid cols={2}>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/numbers1.webp" alt="Option 1: stacked vinyl records for RIAA certifications" caption="Option 1: one vinyl record per certification." />

</div>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/numbers2.webp" alt="Option 2: pie charts for award ratios" caption="Option 2: pie charts for win/nomination ratios." />

</div>

</Grid>

Feedback: the records fit the music theme, but "gold" and "platinum" needed explaining, and the pie charts didn't match the rest of the piece.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Location: where the music was made">

<Grid cols={2}>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/location1.webp" alt="Option 1: dotted world map with studios" caption="Option 1: dotted world map." />

</div>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/location2.webp" alt="Option 2: grouped studios with leading lines" caption="Option 2: grouped studios with leading lines." />

</div>

</Grid>

Feedback: the dot map was fun, but white text on white dots was hard to read; grouped leading lines were cleaner but took more space.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Relationships: genres and collaborators">

<Grid cols={2}>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/relationships1.webp" alt="Option 1: genre wheel" caption="Option 1: layered genre wheel." />

</div>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/relationships3.webp" alt="Option 2: collaborator portraits" caption="Option 2: collaborator portraits." />

</div>

</Grid>

Feedback: watch spacing in the wheel so small genres aren't squeezed, and separate Frank from his collaborators to show the hierarchy.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Time: the discography">

<Grid cols={2}>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/time1.webp" alt="Option 1: works catalog sized by release type" caption="Option 1: art sized by release type." />

</div>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/time2.png" alt="Option 2: left-to-right timeline" caption="Option 2: simple timeline showing the 2014–2016 hiatus." />

</div>

</Grid>

Feedback: the size coding wasn't obvious, and the type was too small; the timeline needed months and titles.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Layout: putting it together">

<Grid cols={2}>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/layout1.webp" alt="Option 1: angled Bauhaus-style layout" caption="Option 1: 30° angled layout." />

</div>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/layout2.webp" alt="Option 2: straight horizontal layout" caption="Option 2: straight layout." />

</div>

</Grid>

Peers were split: the angled version felt more distinctive, and the straight version was easier to read. The final piece uses a horizontal layout built around the circular rings.

</Accordion>
