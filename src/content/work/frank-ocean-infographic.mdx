# Frank Ocean: By the Numbers

Graphic Design

## Project Summary

I created an infographic to effectively convey information about Grammy-award winning singer, song-writer Frank Ocean.<br /><br />I learned to leverage the 5 principles of Visual Potential: Appearance, Relation, Numbers, Time, and Location, and applied visual information design principles such as: Visual potential, Visual language, Visual hierarchy, Use of color (TEACH)

<Eyebrow>Time</Eyebrow>

## Exploiting the Visual Potential of Time

<Rule />

### First Draft

<Img src="frank-ocean/time1.webp" alt="image of frank ocean time model" />

I created this Frank Ocean's 'works' section as a concise catalog of his discography, spanning from the first mixtape to the latest collaborations and singles. <br /><br />Coded 'bullets' indicate album, mixtape, single, or feature, resembling a track listing. The art sizes follow a hierarchy, with the largest for albums/mixtapes, smaller for singles, and the smallest for features, maintaining a square form for cohesion. A legend/key will be added to provide clarity.<br /><br />Dates, in Helvetica type, with specific month/year annotations, create a chronological, left-to-right flow in this organized timeline.

<Img src="frank-ocean/time2.png" alt="image of frank ocean time model" />

This second 'works' option offers a simpler approach. Viewers see various albums, singles, and features on the left-to-right timeline. A noticeable gap from 2014 to 2016 signifies Frank Ocean's sabbatical. <br /><br />Album arts are stacked by year, visually grouping dates. Equal year spacing contrasts the 2014-2016 hiatus with the music-rich other parts of the timeline, presenting a straightforward visual representation.

<Eyebrow>Layout</Eyebrow>

## Exploiting the Visual Potential of Layouts

<Rule />

### First Draft

<Grid cols={2}>

<div>

<Img src="frank-ocean/layout1.webp" alt="image of frank ocean layout model" />

</div>

<div>

## Option #1

**Arrangement:** I set up a top-down, horizontal layout, strategically placing circular rings with "Frank Ocean" to instantly grab attention. Leading lines guide the viewer's eyes from left to right, and the 30-degree slant enhances this directional flow.**<br /><br />Grouping:** Between the medians, I organized blocks with specific information. The top sections cover Frank Ocean's bio details, while the bottom sections delve into less biographic info like collabs and genres. The median 'Works' timeline acts as a meeting point, connecting both halves together.**<br /><br />Size of Elements:** I variated the size of sections to emphasize significance. Larger ones at the top delve into Frank Ocean's history, while smaller ones at the bottom complement with details about personnel, courses, and locations.

</div>

</Grid>
