# Our Stories Are Your Stories (OSAYS)

OSAYS is an AAPI Heritage Month campaign that shares AANHPI stories so people in the community know they're seen, and so others can understand experiences unlike their own. It started for me with a blog post, [I Am The Proud Son Of Refugees](https://www.successcohorts.com/blog/summer-retreat-te8j4). Betti Fujikado, co-founder of Success Cohorts, encouraged me to tell more of my story through OSAYS.

## David and Karen (2021)

<Video vimeo="561430519" title="OSAYS: David and Karen" />

My friend MaryAnne and I spent three weeks, with **no video experience and no budget**, turning my essay into a film for OSAYS and the Wing Luke Museum. Family across Washington, California and France dug through their photo albums for it. Listening to my parents talk about their childhoods during the war made me more empathetic to what they went through, and when the film came out, my family were the first to say how proud they were to see their story told.

## Success Cohorts (2023)

<Video vimeo="828393638" title="OSAYS: Success Cohorts" />

I was in Success Cohorts' first (Alpha) cohort, as a recent grad entering a historically bad pandemic job market. The bi-weekly sessions on goal-setting, accountability and networking helped me reset, and gave me the confidence and connections to land my full-time job. Thank you to Betti, Carla, Linnea and my mentor, Korey.
