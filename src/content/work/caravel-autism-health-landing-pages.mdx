## Overview

Caravel Autism approached Yext with a request to update their facility pages. In the past, Caravel had to manually update each page. **However, Caravel frequently experienced changes in staff, hours, description, and other core facility information. These changes were especially hard to keep track of for over 40 Caravel facilities.**

## Situation

Role: As the lead implementation manager for Yext, I led the consolidation and centralization of all of Caravel’s facility data into one centralized ‘source of truth’ platform, and **led the design, project management, and implementation of 40+ facility pages for Caravel Autism Health’s website.** <br /><br />I closely collaborated with Caravel’s VPs to confirm buy-in on my proposed website design and information architecture. Additionally, I closely collaborated with Yext’s developers and product support specialists to confirm the designs were in scope and function optimally between mobile and desktop breakpoints.

</Section>

<Eyebrow>Problem</Eyebrow>

### Problems for the business

<Rule />

## Problem #1: Vital information is buried below the fold

Customers visit websites to scan, not to peruse. Through our initial kickoff call with Caravel’s VPs, I learned that many sessions were dropping off without any significant CTAs being clicked. Caravel suspected it had to do with how the page was laid out. Our research from other healthcare clients found that the goals for users were to find contact info for ‘general inquiries’, ‘get directions’, and to learn about the staff/specialists. Upon conducting a heuristic review of their website, these key call to action buttons and modules were buried below the fold towards the bottom of the page.

### Problem #2: Manual updates

Customers want to visit a landing page and be 100% confident that the data displayed is factual and up-to-date. After conducting an initial kickoff call with Caravel’s VPs, I learned Caravel had a history of outdated key fields on their facility pages, such as business hours during a holiday, or leaving former staff members on the page. Incorrect data, while easy to change, plagued Caravel’s facility pages if they were not properly updated. Any user friction due to outdated or inaccurate data led to decreased trust for the platform, which led users to directly call the facilities for questions that could have easily been solved with up-to-date pages.

## Solution #1: Highlight vital information

## Solution #2: Centralize and use a 'source of truth'

Upon our initial kickoff call discussions, I noticed that Caravel would update their listings with updates to business hours, descriptions, etc. This meant that if a facility's hour needed to be updated, Caravel would first update the hours on Google, Facebook, and other listings they have online. **Sometimes Caravel would remember to make the same updates on their facility pages, but often they would be updated a few days afterwards, or until the facility contacts the marketing department.**<br /><br />The foundation for building powerful digital website experiences starts with a centralized content management. To make sure this is accomplished with Caravel, I closely collaborated with Caravel’s data team, working with all of their raw facility data for each of their locations, and loading it into Yext’s central source of truth, called the Knowledge Graph. I was able to create real-world entities for each facility, which to Caravel's key publishers (ex: Google, Facebook, Apple), healthcare-centric publishers (ex: WebMD, CareDash), and of course, Caravel’s facility pages. Given the complex nature of Caravel’s data, I created multiple custom fields including for Staff, Serving areas, FAQs, to ensure these fields would also update within the listings and pages.<br /><br />Keeping your data fresh and clean is critical for your brand’s success. If, for example, Caravel’s Green Bay facility needed to update its staff information and location hours, they can quickly update those fields in the Knowledge Graph, and the data will immediately update everywhere in the digital space.
